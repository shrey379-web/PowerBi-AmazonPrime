# 🎬 Amazon Prime Video Content Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to explore Amazon Prime Video's content catalogue, including titles, ratings, genres, directors, content types, geographical distribution, and release-year trends.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

![Amazon Prime Video Dashboard](images/amazon-prime-dashboard.png)

*Dashboard preview showcasing content KPIs, ratings and genre distributions, country-wise content mapping, movie versus TV show comparison, and release-year trends.*

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores Amazon Prime Video's content dataset using Microsoft Power BI to transform raw catalogue data into meaningful visual insights.

The dashboard provides an overview of content distribution across ratings, genres, countries, and release years. It also compares movies and TV shows to help users understand the composition and characteristics of the available catalogue.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the size and composition of the content catalogue.
- Examine content ratings and genre distributions.
- Compare movies and TV shows.
- Explore the geographical distribution of titles.
- Analyze content release-year trends.
- Present key metrics through an interactive dashboard.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Titles:** 9,655
- **Total Ratings:** 25
- **Total Genres:** 11
- **Total Directors:** 5,771
- **Total Duration:** 220

*These values are displayed in the dashboard. Confirm the definitions and units of the underlying measures, particularly Total Genres and Total Duration, before interpreting them as distinct genre counts or total runtime.*

## 🔍 Dashboard Features

### ⭐ 1. Ratings Analysis

- Compares titles across rating categories such as 13+, 16+, All, 18+, R, and PG-13.
- Highlights the distribution of titles by rating.
- Supports exploration of the catalogue's content-rating composition.

### 🎭 2. Genre Analysis

- Visualizes title counts across genre categories.
- Includes categories such as Drama, Comedy, Drama/Suspense, Comedy/Drama, Animation/Kids, and Documentary.
- Helps identify categories with higher representation in the dataset.

### 🌍 3. Country-Wise Content Distribution

- Uses a geographical map to visualize content distribution by country.
- Supports exploration of the catalogue's international representation.
- Helps compare geographical coverage across the available records.

### 🍿 4. Movies vs. TV Shows

- Compares movies and TV shows using a doughnut chart.
- Shows the distribution of the two content types.
- Provides a quick overview of the catalogue's composition.

### 📅 5. Content Release-Year Analysis

- Visualizes the distribution of titles by release year.
- Highlights changes in catalogue representation across different periods.
- Helps explore how the dataset's release-year profile varies over time.

- The dashboard provides a consolidated view of catalogue size and content characteristics.
- Ratings and genre visuals help explore the distribution of available titles.
